THE NUMBERS HAVE DIFFERENT JOBS

A useful estimate.
An intentional plan.

You supply the stock estimate. PrintPlan keeps track of what is already spoken for, so the same recorded grams aren’t promised twice.

↳

Choose the spool that suits the job.

Best-fit suggests an eligible spool based on material, colour and available grams. You can choose another eligible spool before confirming.

↳

Leave room for a safety margin.

The account margin starts at 10% and can be adjusted. Changing it alone leaves existing reservations unchanged.

↳

Keep plans consistent after printing.

If actual consumption leaves too little recorded stock for other reservations, PrintPlan cancels the newest ones first and tells you what changed.

*

Good planning starts with your estimate.

PrintPlan uses stock you enter manually. Availability and spool suggestions depend on those estimates; they don’t guarantee that the physical filament will be sufficient.

BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

A few practical answers.

Does PrintPlan connect to my printer?

No. Stock, print requirements and actual consumption are entered manually. There is no automatic printer tracking or G-code / 3MF import.

Does reserving filament reduce my stock?

No. A reservation holds recorded grams for a planned print. Recorded stock is reduced when you settle the completed print with its actual consumption.

What happens if I cancel a reservation?

The held grams become available again. If the print already consumed material, correct recorded stock separately.

Who is this workspace for?

A hobbyist with a personal filament collection who wants to check material before a specific print. It is not a printer-farm management system.
