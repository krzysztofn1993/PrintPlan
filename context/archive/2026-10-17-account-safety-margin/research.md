## Research Question

What does roadmap S-04 require, how does the current reservation workflow support it, and which persistence, security, concurrency, interface and verification contracts must planning address?

The supplied `s-04` resolves to the roadmap's canonical change ID, `account-safety-margin`. Its prerequisite S-03 is marked done; its outcome is account-margin configuration without resizing existing reservations. Reserved-print recalculation belongs to S-05. [Roadmap:138](../../foundation/roadmap.md#L138), [S-04:148](../../foundation/roadmap.md#L148), [S-05:160](../../foundation/roadmap.md#L160)

## Summary

- The product policy is settled: an account margin in whole percentage points from 0% through 100%, defaulting to 10%; round adjusted requirements upward to whole grams. With an existing 88 g reservation for an 80 g print at 10%, changing the account setting to 5% leaves that reservation at 88 g. A new 80 g print requires 84 g if eligible stock is sufficient. Best-fit and manual selection use the same current setting. [PRD US-02:61](../../foundation/prd.md#L61), [FR-008:105](../../foundation/prd.md#L105), [arithmetic:121](../../foundation/prd.md#L121)
- In the inspected migrations and application paths, there is no account-settings persistence or editor. Preview calculation, preview margin output, confirmation calculation and reservation margin insertion currently use literal `10`. The numeric calculation helper and committed reservation snapshots are already suitable seams. [Preview:229](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L229), [confirmation:258](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L258), [types:5](../../../src/types/database.ts#L5), [current dashboard:7](../../../src/pages/dashboard.astro#L7)
- The consequential gap is stale confirmation: a reservation command carries the print version but no evidence of the displayed margin. Replacing literals with account reads could silently reserve a different amount from the preview when the selected spool remains eligible. Planning must settle the stale-margin confirmation policy while preserving current-margin calculation. [Browser payload:351](../../../src/components/prints/PrintList.tsx#L351), [validation:33](../../../src/lib/reservation-validation.ts#L33), [database checks:267](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L267)
- Existing owner serialization, authenticated RPC writes, stored reservation grams and layered verification can be extended. A new account setting needs its own persistence, validation, read/save/recovery contracts and upgrade coverage; automatic reserved-print recalculation stays in S-05. [Mutex:55](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L55), [stored totals:81](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L81), [roadmap:160](../../foundation/roadmap.md#L160)

## Detailed Findings

### Product rules and scope

The terms account safety margin, planned print, spool, reservation, recorded stock and available grams come from the PRD's requirements and Business Logic. No domain glossary was present in the inspected context inventory; this research introduces no aggregate terminology. [PRD:82](../../foundation/prd.md#L82), [Business Logic:115](../../foundation/prd.md#L115)

The margin policy, integer range, rounding and preservation of existing reservations require no new product interview. Per-print margin settings and automatic recalculation of the queue remain excluded. Editing a reserved print using the current margin is a later slice; the current cancellation-before-requirement-edit guard remains applicable during S-04. [PRD:119](../../foundation/prd.md#L119), [Non-Goals:139](../../foundation/prd.md#L139), [S-05:160](../../foundation/roadmap.md#L160), [current edit guard:192](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L192)

Acceptance examples must state stock conditions. With the PRD's single 150 g spool and an existing 88 g reservation, 62 g remains available, which cannot accommodate another 84 g reservation after changing to 5%. Demonstrating the new 84 g amount requires additional eligible stock or release of the earlier reservation; reducing the margin must not free grams from that earlier reservation. For an 81 g requirement at 5%, the settled upward-rounding rule produces 86 g. [US-01:47](../../foundation/prd.md#L47), [US-02:61](../../foundation/prd.md#L61), [formula:121](../../foundation/prd.md#L121)

### Persistence and applied-margin snapshots

The inspected schema consists of the spool, print and reservation migrations. The reservation migration adds active reservations, a private owner mutex and a private successful-request ledger. It does not define an account-margin record. The hand-maintained database types expose print/spool tables, reservation-aware views and reservation RPCs, without a settings contract. [Spool schema:1](../../../supabase/migrations/20261010000000_create_spools.sql#L1), [print schema:1](../../../supabase/migrations/20261012000000_create_prints.sql#L1), [reservation schema:7](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L7), [private tables:36](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L36), [types:5](../../../src/types/database.ts#L5)

The helper already calculates `ceil(required_grams * (100 + margin_percent) / 100)` with database numeric arithmetic. Preview passes 10 and returns 10; reserve passes 10 and inserts 10. Best-fit and manual confirmation share that calculation before their selection-specific checks. Account settings should become the authoritative input at these seams rather than adding browser arithmetic. This is an integration recommendation, not an implemented change. [Helper:69](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L69), [preview:239](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L239), [reserve:282](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L282)

An active reservation stores its own `reserved_grams`, `margin_percent`, selection mode and creation time. Inventory sums stored reservation grams; the planned-print projection exposes the stored grams. Therefore a setting update can preserve existing reservations by leaving these rows untouched. The existing reservation-margin constraint checks the numeric range but not integrality; it does not supply the whole-percentage validation required for the new account setting. [Snapshot constraints:7](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L7), [projections:81](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L81)

Planning needs an effective-default strategy for existing and newly registered accounts. A dedicated owner-keyed setting with a default read for a missing row is one possible design; eager initialization is another. Whichever is selected must retain 10% behavior before the account has saved a preference, distinguish a failed read from an absent preference, and enforce the whole-percentage range at the database and HTTP boundaries. These are requirements derived from FR-008 and the existing database-authority pattern, not a selected schema. [FR-008:105](../../foundation/prd.md#L105), [owner derivation and mutation boundary:258](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L258)

The previous plan explicitly preserves large adjusted requirements: with a 2,147,483,647 g input and a 100% margin, demand is 4,294,967,294 g. This exceeds the valid stock of a single spool, so it must remain representable and yield insufficient material rather than overflow or a reduced margin. The current reservation constraint and browser DTO parser already accommodate that demand bound. [Accepted bounds:67](../../archive/2026-10-16-reserve-and-release-material/plan.md#L67), [SQL bound:16](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L16), [DTO bound:38](../../../src/lib/print-dto.ts#L38)

### Serialization, stale previews and retries

The owner mutex inserts an owner row if absent and locks it with `FOR UPDATE`. Reserve acquires that lock before reading its request ledger, print, eligibility and totals. The accepted S-03 design requires mutation functions to read state in statements after lock acquisition under Read Committed. A settings save should join this protocol, with confirmation reading the effective margin after locking, so setting updates and reservation creation have an ordered outcome. Runtime proof remains implementation work. [Mutex:55](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L55), [reserve sequencing:267](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L267), [accepted protocol:61](../../archive/2026-10-16-reserve-and-release-material/plan.md#L61)

Preview is an advisory `STABLE` function returning print version, margin, adjusted grams, suggestion and eligible spools. Confirmation currently receives request ID, print ID/version, spool ID and selection mode; it does not receive expected margin or settings revision. It checks the print version and current spool eligibility, with `selection_stale` when the best-fit spool changes. [Preview:229](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L229), [confirmation:258](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L258), [browser payload:351](../../../src/components/prints/PrintList.tsx#L351)

**Inference:** after replacing the fixed margin with a settings read, an account change between preview and confirmation could change reserved grams without changing the print version or selected spool. The present checks would not identify that difference. Serializing writes prevents conflicting stock calculations; it does not establish that the user reviewed the committed amount. A stale-margin check must apply to best-fit and manual reservations. Honoring an obsolete preview margin for a fresh reservation would conflict with the current-setting rule. [Current checks:276](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L276), [PRD:119](../../foundation/prd.md#L119)

For planning, requiring a fresh preview and explicit reconfirmation is a compatible candidate. Decide whether the command witnesses expected margin, a settings revision or another preview token; expected value accepts change-away-and-back, while a revision can detect intervening saves. Coordinate any payload change across the browser, exact-field validator, service, RPC and request ledger. The witness must validate freshness; the server still supplies the calculation margin. [Exact field set:33](../../../src/lib/reservation-validation.ts#L33), [service arguments:142](../../../src/lib/reservations.ts#L142), [ledger payload:40](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L40)

Successful-request replay is a different operation from a fresh reservation. The current reserve path checks an existing matching ledger entry before recalculation and returns its existing active reservation ID. Released/deleted requests conflict rather than recreate reservations. Preserve this ordering when introducing freshness checks: retrying a previously committed request after changing the account margin must not resize the existing reservation or reject it merely because the account setting subsequently changed. If a new witness enters request identity, retain the original request parameters for comparison. [Replay branch:268](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L268), [ledger states:40](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L40), [US-02:61](../../foundation/prd.md#L61)

### Ownership and authenticated boundaries

The inspected reservation functions derive ownership from `auth.uid()`, use qualified relations with an empty search path and restrict execution to authenticated callers. Direct authenticated product-table writes are revoked; owner-scoped SELECT remains available, with RLS on reservations and security-invoker projections. A new settings write must not introduce a direct-write path that bypasses the shared locking protocol. [Owner check:263](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L263), [RLS:30](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L30), [read views:81](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L81), [privileges:333](../../../supabase/migrations/20261016000000_reserve_and_release_material.sql#L333)

The request middleware initializes the session client and verifies the user. Dashboard descendants receive protected-page handling; the reservation API independently handles unavailable auth and missing identity before parsing the mutation. A settings API should derive its owner from verified identity and follow the private response/cache conventions. A settings failure needs setting-specific copy rather than reusing print failure text unchanged. [Middleware:9](../../../src/lib/auth-middleware.ts#L9), [protected paths:3](../../../src/lib/auth-response.ts#L3), [API checks:15](../../../src/pages/api/prints/[id]/reservation/reserve.ts#L15), [private JSON:5](../../../src/lib/reservation-response.ts#L5)

Current Supabase documentation was fetched through Context7 using `/supabase/supabase`. It supports explicitly qualifying relations when a definer function has an empty search path, owner-scoped authenticated RLS, and controlling definer-function execution privileges. These references support the existing security pattern; they do not prove the behavior of a future settings RPC. [Database functions](https://github.com/supabase/supabase/blob/master/apps/docs/content/guides/database/functions.mdx), [RLS](https://github.com/supabase/supabase/blob/master/apps/docs/content/guides/database/postgres/row-level-security.mdx)

### Interface and recovery contracts

The inspected dashboard, navigation, dashboard routes and application helper/component inventory contain no account-settings surface. An editor on the dashboard or a dedicated settings page are feasible choices; location and save interaction are not specified in the PRD. Reuse `DashboardShell`, shared `FormField`, `Button` and Carbon form/feedback classes. [Dashboard:7](../../../src/pages/dashboard.astro#L7), [navigation:21](../../../src/components/Topbar.astro#L21), [shell:15](../../../src/layouts/DashboardShell.astro#L15), [field:29](../../../src/components/ui/FormField.tsx#L29), [styles:489](../../../src/styles/global.css#L489)

The reservation panel already renders the returned percentage and adjusted grams. Existing reservations render stored `reservedGrams` independently. Showing the historical applied percentage would require a DTO/view extension, but preserving current stored-gram display does not. Update the route's fixed-default wording when configurability ships. The browser preview parser checks numeric range without checking integer percentages; settings validation must enforce the stronger product rule. [Preview display:90](../../../src/components/prints/ReservationPanel.tsx#L90), [existing reservation:39](../../../src/components/prints/ReservationPanel.tsx#L39), [current copy:39](../../../src/pages/dashboard/prints.astro#L39), [parser:286](../../../src/components/prints/PrintList.tsx#L286), [DTO:30](../../../src/lib/print-dto.ts#L30)

Preview requests already reject obsolete responses by request counter and active print. A fresh preview resets selection to best-fit; clicking a spool records manual mode. A same-page setting save should invalidate an open preview; a different page/tab still needs server confirmation checks. Decide whether stale-margin recovery retains an eligible manual choice or follows the current reset behavior. [Request guard:254](../../../src/components/prints/PrintList.tsx#L254), [selection reset:303](../../../src/components/prints/PrintList.tsx#L303), [manual choice:831](../../../src/components/prints/PrintList.tsx#L831)

The existing `selection_stale` error and recovery copy refer specifically to a changed best-fit spool. A margin conflict needs explicit semantics and fresh-preview recovery rather than falling into generic conflict handling with an actionable stale preview. Network failures, unreadable success and server errors currently guard reservation mutations until authoritative refresh; settings save needs an equivalent distinction between rejected input and uncertain persistence. Retain the draft and reread saved settings after an uncertain result; do not alter displayed reservation grams optimistically. [Error mapping:31](../../../src/lib/reservation-response.ts#L31), [confirmation/recovery:361](../../../src/components/prints/PrintList.tsx#L361), [conflict handling:391](../../../src/components/prints/PrintList.tsx#L391), [refresh:220](../../../src/components/prints/PrintList.tsx#L220)

## Architecture Insights and Affected Contracts

Keep authoritative settings reads and reservation calculation in the database, serialize settings writes with existing owner mutations, and preserve committed reservation snapshots. Application changes span a settings read/save contract and editor, database types, and potentially the reservation confirmation payload/freshness errors. Existing stock and print DTO quantities should retain their meanings. The current print DTO contract explicitly enumerates public fields, so expanding it should be deliberate rather than incidental.
